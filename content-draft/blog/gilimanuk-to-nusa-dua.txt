### H1
Gilimanuk to Nusa Dua Private Transfer

### Quick Answer (AI citation block — 40–80 words, place directly under H1)
Book a private transfer from Gilimanuk to Nusa Dua with Bali West Transport. The journey covers approximately 148 km and takes around 4.5 to 5.5 hours depending on traffic, crossing Bali from the western ferry port to the island's premier resort enclave. Travel by comfortable private car door-to-door straight to your hotel in Nusa Dua — ideal for travellers arriving from Java via ferry and heading to South Bali's luxury resorts.

### Why Choose This Transfer
Nusa Dua is a master-planned resort district on the southeastern tip of the Bukit Peninsula, home to some of Bali's most prestigious international hotel brands. It's a long, cross-island drive from Gilimanuk — far more comfortable in a private, air-conditioned vehicle than switching between buses and taxis. Our drivers know the route through Negara, Tabanan, and the Denpasar bypass well, and are familiar with Nusa Dua's gated resort access points. Door-to-door service means no juggling luggage between ferry, bus terminal, and taxi — one booking, one driver, straight to your Nusa Dua resort.

### Distance and Travel Time (AI citation block)
The distance between Gilimanuk and Nusa Dua is approximately 148 km by road, with a private car journey taking around 4.5 to 5.5 hours depending on traffic conditions and time of day. This is one of Bali West Transport's longest transfer routes, crossing the island from the western ferry port to the southeastern Bukit Peninsula. Afternoon departures through Denpasar can add extra time due to local traffic congestion.

### Pickup Locations
- Gilimanuk Ferry Port (Pelabuhan Gilimanuk)
- Gilimanuk town and surrounding hotels
- Karangsewu Beach area
- Custom pickup points along the Java–Bali ferry crossing on request

### Drop-off Locations
- Nusa Dua Beach (Pantai Mengiat) and international resort hotels
- Geger Beach
- Tanjung Benoa
- Bali Collection shopping and dining complex
- Bali Nusa Dua Convention Centre (BNDCC)
- Pandawa Beach area (nearby, available on request)
- All major Nusa Dua resorts (St. Regis, Ritz-Carlton, Conrad, Kempinski, Grand Hyatt, Sofitel, Mulia, Westin and more)

### Who Uses This Transfer
- Travellers arriving via the Gilimanuk–Ketapang ferry from Java heading directly to a Nusa Dua resort
- Couples and honeymooners booking Nusa Dua's luxury beachfront hotels
- Families wanting a calm, gated resort area with white sand beaches and calm swimming water
- Conference and event attendees heading to the Bali Nusa Dua Convention Centre
- Groups relocating from West Bali (Menjangan, West Bali National Park) to South Bali's resort strip
- Anyone wanting a single, stress-free door-to-door ride instead of multiple bus/ferry connections

### Journey Overview
From Gilimanuk, the route heads east along Bali's main cross-island road through Negara and Tabanan, skirting rice terraces and small towns before joining the Denpasar bypass. From there, it continues south through Jimbaran and into the Bukit Peninsula, arriving at Nusa Dua's landscaped resort enclave — a striking contrast to the rural roads crossed earlier in the journey. Given the distance, we recommend an early departure to avoid Denpasar's afternoon traffic and arrive at your resort in good time to enjoy the rest of the day.

### Benefits of Private Transfer
- No transfers between ferry, bus, and taxi — one direct ride
- Air-conditioned comfort for one of Bali's longest domestic transfer routes
- Direct drop-off at your resort's gated entrance — no need to arrange a separate onward taxi
- Optional stops (GWK Cultural Park, Tanah Lot, Medewi Surf Beach) available on request
- English-speaking drivers familiar with the Gilimanuk–Nusa Dua route
- Fixed door-to-door service — no haggling on arrival

The journey takes approximately 4.5 to 5.5 hours by private car, depending on traffic through Denpasar and into the Bukit Peninsula.

Nusa Dua is approximately 148 km from Gilimanuk Ferry Port, making it one of Bali West Transport's longest transfer routes.

Yes. Popular stops include GWK Cultural Park, Tanah Lot Temple, and Medewi Surf Beach. Let us know your preferred stops when booking.

We provide direct drop-off at all major Nusa Dua resorts, including St. Regis, Ritz-Carlton, Conrad, Kempinski, Grand Hyatt, Sofitel, Mulia, and Westin, as well as Tanjung Benoa and Geger Beach hotels.
