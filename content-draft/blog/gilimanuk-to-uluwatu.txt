## 2. Page Copy — EN Version

### H1
Gilimanuk to Uluwatu Private Transfer

### Quick Answer (AI citation block — 40–80 words, place directly under H1)
Book a private transfer from Gilimanuk to Uluwatu with Bali West Transport. The journey covers approximately 145 km and takes around 4.5 to 5.5 hours depending on traffic, making it the longest route in our network. Travel by comfortable private car door-to-door from Gilimanuk Ferry Port straight to your hotel, villa, or beach club in Uluwatu — ideal for travellers arriving from Java via ferry.

### Why Choose This Transfer
Uluwatu is Bali's furthest-south destination, on the tip of the Bukit Peninsula — a long, multi-region drive that's far more comfortable in a private, air-conditioned vehicle than by public bus with multiple transfers. Our drivers know the route through Negara, Tabanan, and the Denpasar bypass well, including the best rest stops along the way. Door-to-door service means no juggling luggage between ferry, bus terminal, and taxi — one booking, one driver, straight to Uluwatu.

### Distance and Travel Time (AI citation block)
The distance between Gilimanuk and Uluwatu is approximately 145 km by road, with a private car journey taking around 4.5 to 5.5 hours depending on traffic conditions and time of day. This is Bali West Transport's longest transfer route, crossing the island from the western ferry port to the southern Bukit Peninsula. Afternoon departures through Denpasar and into the Bukit can add extra time due to local traffic congestion.

### Pickup Locations
- Gilimanuk Ferry Port (Pelabuhan Gilimanuk)
- Gilimanuk town and surrounding hotels
- Karangsewu Beach area
- Custom pickup points along the Java–Bali ferry crossing on request

### Drop-off Locations
- Uluwatu Temple (Pura Luhur Uluwatu) area
- Padang Padang Beach
- Bingin Beach
- Suluban Beach (Blue Point)
- Balangan Beach
- Melasti Beach
- Green Bowl Beach
- Pecatu and surrounding villas/hotels
- GWK Cultural Park (en route stop available)

### Who Uses This Transfer
- Travellers arriving via the Gilimanuk–Ketapang ferry from Java heading straight to Uluwatu's beaches and resorts
- Surfers heading to Uluwatu, Padang Padang, Bingin, or Suluban's world-class breaks
- Couples and honeymooners booking clifftop villas in Pecatu and the Bukit Peninsula
- Groups relocating from West Bali (Menjangan, West Bali National Park) to South Bali's beach clubs
- Anyone wanting a single, stress-free door-to-door ride instead of multiple bus/ferry connections

### Journey Overview
From Gilimanuk, the route heads east along Bali's main cross-island road through Negara and Tabanan, skirting rice terraces and small towns before joining the Denpasar bypass. From there, it continues south through Jimbaran and onto the Bukit Peninsula, where the roads narrow and climb toward Uluwatu's clifftop terrain. Given the distance, we recommend an early departure to avoid Denpasar's afternoon traffic and arrive in Uluwatu in good time for sunset.

### Benefits of Private Transfer
- No transfers between ferry, bus, and taxi — one direct ride
- Air-conditioned comfort for Bali's longest domestic transfer route
- Flexible pickup and drop-off points, including remote Bukit clifftop villas
- Optional stops (GWK Cultural Park, Tanah Lot, Medewi Surf Beach) available on request
- English-speaking drivers familiar with the Gilimanuk–Uluwatu route
- Fixed door-to-door service — no haggling on arrival

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQPage schema source)
**How long does the Gilimanuk to Uluwatu transfer take?**
The journey takes approximately 4.5 to 5.5 hours by private car, depending on traffic through Denpasar and into the Bukit Peninsula.

**How far is Uluwatu from Gilimanuk?**
Uluwatu is approximately 145 km from Gilimanuk Ferry Port, making it Bali West Transport's longest single transfer route.

**Can I stop along the way?**
Yes. Popular stops include GWK Cultural Park, Tanah Lot Temple, and Medewi Surf Beach. Let us know your preferred stops when booking.

**Where in Uluwatu can I be dropped off?**
We serve all main areas including Uluwatu Temple, Padang Padang, Bingin, Suluban, Balangan, Melasti, and Pecatu villas and hotels.

**How do I book this transfer?**
Contact us directly via WhatsApp with your ferry arrival time, passenger count, and drop-off address in Uluwatu to receive your best offer.

**Is this transfer suitable for surfboards or extra luggage?**
Yes, let us know in advance if you're travelling with surfboards or extra luggage so we can arrange a suitable vehicle.
