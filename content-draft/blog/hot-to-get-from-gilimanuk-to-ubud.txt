### H2: Option 1 — Public Bus (Cheapest)

The public bus is the cheapest way to get from Gilimanuk to Ubud. PT Putra Sehati Transport and Po Putra Abadi run buses from Terminal Gilimanuk to Terminal Ubung (Denpasar) every few hours. From Ubung, you catch another bus or a Grab/Gojek to Ubud.

**How it works:**
1. Walk to Terminal Gilimanuk — it's about 500 metres from the ferry exit.
2. Board a bus bound for Terminal Ubung (Denpasar). Journey: ~4 hours 26 minutes.
3. At Ubung, catch a Trans Metro Dewata bus (free) or a Grab to central Ubud. Journey: ~45–60 minutes.

**Cost breakdown:**
- Gilimanuk → Ubung: Rp 60,000–90,000
- Ubung → Ubud (Grab/Gojek): Rp 50,000–80,000
- **Total: ~Rp 110,000–170,000 (~$7–11 USD)**

**The catch:** Buses at Gilimanuk sometimes wait to fill seats before departing. Local minibuses ("Bis Umum") stop at every village along the route and extend your travel time significantly — avoid those if you see them. Buy a ticket for the express or VIP/Executive service specifically.

**Pro tip:** Don't buy tickets from touts at the ferry exit. Walk to the terminal directly and buy at the counter.

---

### H2: Option 2 — Tourist Shuttle (Best Middle Ground)

Shared tourist shuttles run between Gilimanuk and Ubud and are the most popular choice for solo travellers and pairs. Companies like Perama Tour, Kura Kura Bus, and various local operators run these routes, though schedules change seasonally.

**What you get:**
- Air-conditioned minivan or small bus
- Mostly direct or one-stop route to Ubud
- Hotel pick-up (if pre-booked in advance) or central meeting point
- Fixed departure times — no waiting for the bus to fill

**Cost:** Rp 150,000–250,000 per person (~$9–16 USD)

**Travel time:** 3.5–4.5 hours depending on traffic

**How to book:** Book through 12Go Asia, Bookaway, or directly through your accommodation in advance. Walk-up tickets at Gilimanuk are available but limited — especially during peak season (July–August).

**One limitation:** Shuttle pick-up points can be inflexible. If your ferry is delayed, you may miss your slot. Consider booking the next departure time to give yourself a buffer.

---

### H2: Option 3 — Private Transfer (Fastest, Most Comfortable)

A private car with driver is the fastest and most direct way from Gilimanuk to Ubud. Your driver meets you at the ferry exit, loads your bags, and takes you straight to your hotel — no transfers, no waiting.

**Cost:** Rp 900,000–1,300,000 (~$56–80 USD) for the whole vehicle (up to 4–6 passengers)

**Travel time:** 3.5–4.5 hours (traffic-dependent; can reach 4.5–5 hours if you leave mid-morning)

**What's included (varies by operator):**
- Air-conditioned car (usually Toyota Avanza, Innova, or equivalent)
- English-speaking driver
- Door-to-door service
- Flexibility for roadside stops

**Where to book:**
- GetYourGuide, Viator, Daytrip (prepay online — reliable)
- Directly via WhatsApp with local drivers (slightly cheaper, requires trust)
- Your hotel can usually arrange this if you call ahead

**Optional stops along the route:**
Many private transfer operators offer sightseeing stops at no extra transport charge (entrance fees are separate):
- **Medewi Surf Beach** — relaxed beach, local warungs, worth 30 minutes
- **Jatiluwih Rice Terraces** — UNESCO-listed, adds ~45 minutes to the journey
- **Tanah Lot Temple** — iconic sea temple, best at sunset, ~15 min detour off the main route
- **Pura Luhur Batukaru** — mountain temple, peaceful, less crowded than Tanah Lot

If you're not in a rush, the private transfer with two stops makes this one of the best value drives in Bali.

### H2: Where Exactly Is Gilimanuk, and Why Are You There?

Gilimanuk is a small port town on the far western tip of Bali, approximately 127 km from Ubud. It's not a tourist destination itself — it's a transit point. Ferries run 24 hours a day across the Bali Strait to Ketapang (East Java), making Gilimanuk the entry point for overland travellers coming from Java.

Most people arrive in Gilimanuk after:
- Crossing from Ketapang (Banyuwangi) by ferry (~30 minutes)
- Completing a Java trip (Kawah Ijen, Bromo, Yogyakarta) and heading to Bali
- An overland trip from Surabaya, Malang, or Yogyakarta

Once you arrive at Gilimanuk Ferry Port, you exit the terminal and immediately face a line of taxi and transport offers. This is normal — and usually fine — but prices are inflated at the exit. Walk 200–500 metres and you'll find the public bus terminal and more reasonable quotes.

---

### H2: Tips for Getting from Gilimanuk to Ubud Without Getting Ripped Off

1. **Don't accept the first taxi price at the ferry exit.** Drivers here quote 2–3x the standard rate to arriving passengers. Walk toward the terminal.

2. **Avoid "Bis Umum" (local minibuses).** These buses circle through every village and can add 2+ hours to your journey. Book an express or VIP bus specifically.

3. **Use Grab or Gojek from Ubung.** If you take the public bus to Ubung Terminal (Denpasar), rideshare apps give you a fixed, fair price to Ubud. Much better than negotiating with terminal taxis.

4. **Pre-book your transfer if arriving late.** Gilimanuk's ferry runs 24 hours, but driver availability at 2 AM is limited. Pre-booking via GetYourGuide or WhatsApp the day before avoids being stranded.

5. **Allow for ferry delay buffer.** Bali Strait crossings are usually ~30 minutes, but loading vehicles onto the ferry (especially motorbikes and trucks) adds time. Plan to be at Gilimanuk 15–20 minutes after the ferry docks, not immediately.

6. **Book the shuttle for the correct day.** Shuttle departure times in Gilimanuk are fixed. If your ferry is late, you may miss your slot. Always book the departure after your expected ferry arrival, not the same time.

### H2: Frequently Asked Questions

**How far is Gilimanuk from Ubud?**
The road distance from Gilimanuk to Ubud is approximately 127 km (79 miles), following the main coastal highway along Bali's south coast.

**How long does the drive from Gilimanuk to Ubud take?**
Expect 3.5 to 5 hours depending on departure time and traffic. Early morning departures (before 7 AM) consistently take 3.5 hours. Mid-morning departures (9 AM–noon) regularly reach 5 hours due to traffic between Tabanan and Mengwi.

**Is there a direct bus from Gilimanuk to Ubud?**
No direct bus goes from Gilimanuk straight to Ubud. The public bus route goes Gilimanuk → Terminal Ubung (Denpasar), then you transfer onward to Ubud by rideshare or a second bus. Tourist shuttles operate near-direct routes with one or no transfer stops.

**What is the cheapest way to get from Gilimanuk to Ubud?**
The public bus from Terminal Gilimanuk to Terminal Ubung costs around Rp 60,000–90,000. Add Rp 50,000–80,000 for a Grab/Gojek from Ubung to Ubud. Total budget option: ~Rp 110,000–170,000 (around $7–11 USD).

**What is the fastest way to get from Gilimanuk to Ubud?**
Private transfer is the fastest option at 3.5–4.5 hours with no waiting or transfers. Tourist shuttles are comparable in speed at 3.5–4.5 hours. The public bus route, including the Ubung transfer, typically takes 5+ hours.

**Can I take a motorbike or scooter from Gilimanuk to Ubud?**
Yes. The ride from Gilimanuk to Ubud takes 3–4 hours by motorbike on the main coastal road. It's a straightforward route. Check your rental agreement — some Bali rental operators restrict one-way or inter-island travel.

**How much does a private transfer from Gilimanuk to Ubud cost?**
Private car transfers from Gilimanuk to Ubud cost approximately Rp 900,000–1,300,000 (~$56–80 USD) for the whole vehicle. The price covers up to 4–6 passengers, making it economical for groups. Book in advance through platforms like GetYourGuide, Viator, or Daytrip.
