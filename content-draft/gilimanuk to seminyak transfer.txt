## Section 1 — Why Choose This Transfer

Arriving at Gilimanuk after the Ketapang–Gilimanuk ferry crossing leaves you at the far western tip of Bali, with no direct public transport to Seminyak. Shared shuttles require connections at Ubung bus terminal in Denpasar, adding transfers, waiting time, and uncertainty to an already long journey.

A private transfer removes every one of those variables. Your driver is at the port when you arrive, your luggage goes straight into the vehicle, and you travel directly to your accommodation without stops or route changes.

Seminyak sits roughly 130–135 km southeast of Gilimanuk on Bali's south coast. The drive follows the island's main western corridor through Negara and Tabanan before reaching the congested southern belt around Kerobokan. Traffic on the final approach — particularly along Sunset Road and through the Kerobokan–Petitenget stretch — can add 30 to 60 minutes during afternoon peak hours. A local driver who knows the side-street alternatives makes a measurable difference on that last leg.

| Distance | Approximately 130–135 km by road |

The route passes through Negara — a good point for a comfort break if needed — then continues east through Tabanan before descending into Greater Denpasar. The final 20 km through Kerobokan into Seminyak is the slowest section of the drive.

## Section 3 — Pickup Locations

Your driver meets you at **Gilimanuk Ferry Port (Pelabuhan Gilimanuk)**, on the western side of the harbour as you disembark. Send us your estimated arrival time and ferry booking reference when you book — we monitor the crossing and adjust for delays so you're never left waiting.

Additional pickup is available from:
- Hotels and guesthouses in Gilimanuk town (if you stayed overnight before crossing)
- Ketapang Port area on the Java side (for pre-arranged, cross-strait itineraries)

---

## Section 4 — Drop-off Locations

Door-to-door delivery to any address in Seminyak, including:
- Jl. Raya Seminyak and Jl. Kayu Jati hotel corridor
- Petitenget (W Bali, Katamama, Potato Head area)
- Oberoi Street and surrounds
- Batu Belig area
- Kerobokan (including villas and properties along Jl. Raya Kerobokan)
- Legian (on request, same zone)

If your drop-off is in a narrow lane or private villa complex, confirm the exact address when booking — our drivers are familiar with Seminyak's one-way road network and will find the right entrance.

## Section 5 — Who Uses This Transfer

**Travellers crossing from Java** — arriving via the Ketapang–Gilimanuk ferry from Banyuwangi, Surabaya, or Ijen. Private transfer is the most direct onward option from the harbour.

**Families with luggage** — Seminyak's hotel and villa market skews toward families and couples. A private vehicle handles multiple suitcases without the weight restrictions of shared shuttles.

**Couples on a resort stay** — Seminyak's Petitenget and Oberoi areas are Bali's honeymoon and boutique-hotel strip. Most guests here expect a seamless arrival, not a 45-minute wait at Ubung bus terminal.

**Surf travellers** — Seminyak, Batu Belig, and Kerobokan are five minutes from Berawa and Batu Bolong beaches. Surfboard transport is accommodated in larger vehicles — confirm board size when booking.

**Tour groups completing a Java–Bali overland itinerary** — if your group crossed from East Java and Seminyak is the first stop, a private vehicle with confirmed capacity is more reliable than coordinating multiple shared rides.

## Section 6 — Journey Overview

The drive from Gilimanuk to Seminyak follows Bali's main west-coast highway for most of its length. After leaving the harbour, the route heads south-east through the flat rice-field plains of Jembrana Regency, passing through Negara — the regional capital and a natural rest stop — before continuing into Tabanan Regency.

From Tabanan the road joins the Greater Denpasar network. Traffic densifies progressively as you approach Kerobokan, and the final kilometres into Seminyak — particularly around the Sunset Road intersection and along Jl. Petitenget — are the busiest stretch of the entire journey.

Your driver will use local route knowledge on the Kerobokan approach to manage the final section. If you need a comfort stop, Negara is the best point — approximately halfway, with reliable rest stop options and fuel available.

---

## Section 7 — Benefits of Private Transfer

**No connections.** Shared transport from Gilimanuk to Seminyak requires at least one change at Ubung terminal in Denpasar. Private transfer is point-to-point with no intermediate stops unless you request them.

**Your schedule, not the bus schedule.** Depart as soon as you step off the ferry — no waiting for a shared vehicle to fill.

**Door-to-door to your villa.** Seminyak's villa-heavy accommodation market means many guests are staying on unmarked lanes. Your driver delivers you to the entrance, not to a drop-off point nearby.

**Luggage flexibility.** Families and long-stay travellers routinely travel with more than the shuttle weight allowance allows. A private vehicle carries what your group brings.

**WhatsApp-first booking and contact.** Confirm your journey, share your ferry arrival time, and track your driver — all via WhatsApp before and during the trip.

## Section 8 — Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does the transfer from Gilimanuk to Seminyak take?**
Approximately 3.5 to 4.5 hours by private vehicle. The drive itself is around 3 hours in clear conditions, but traffic on the Kerobokan–Petitenget approach adds 30 to 60 minutes, particularly between 14:00 and 19:00. Morning ferry arrivals typically have the fastest journey times.

**How far is Gilimanuk from Seminyak?**
Approximately 130–135 km by road, following the main western corridor through Negara and Tabanan before reaching Greater Denpasar and Seminyak.

**Where does the driver meet me at Gilimanuk?**
At Gilimanuk Ferry Port (Pelabuhan Gilimanuk), on the western side of the harbour as you disembark from the Ketapang crossing. Send us your ferry reference and estimated arrival time when booking — we track the crossing and adjust if it runs late.

**Is there a direct bus from Gilimanuk to Seminyak?**
There is no direct bus. Public bus from Gilimanuk goes to Ubung terminal in Denpasar, where you need a second connection to Seminyak. Total travel time including waiting and transfers typically exceeds 5–6 hours. A private transfer is faster and door-to-door.

**Can you transport a surfboard from Gilimanuk to Seminyak?**
Yes. Confirm the number and length of boards when booking so we assign a vehicle with adequate space. Standard MPV fits boards up to around 6'8" with the rear seat folded; longer boards may need a larger vehicle.

**Do you offer early morning pickups from the ferry port?**
Yes. We operate around the Ketapang–Gilimanuk ferry schedule, including the earliest morning crossings. Confirm your ferry departure time when booking and we'll have your driver ready on arrival.

**What is the route from Gilimanuk to Seminyak?**
Gilimanuk Ferry Port → Jl. Raya Gilimanuk south-east → Negara → Tabanan → Kerobokan → Seminyak. The route stays on Bali's main western highway for most of the journey before entering the Greater Denpasar network.

**Can I make a stop along the way, like at Tanah Lot?**
Yes, on request. Tanah Lot is approximately 45 minutes before Seminyak and sits close to the main route. A planned stop adds to the total journey time — confirm with us when booking so your driver can adjust the schedule.
