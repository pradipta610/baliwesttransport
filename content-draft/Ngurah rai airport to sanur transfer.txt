Most travellers heading to Sanur choose a private airport transfer because it offers fixed pricing, luggage assistance, and avoids negotiating with airport taxis. Travel time is typically around 30 minutes but can increase during peak traffic periods.

Why Stay in Sanur?

Sanur is one of Bali's most established beach destinations. Unlike the busy nightlife areas of Seminyak and Canggu, Sanur offers a relaxed atmosphere, family-friendly beaches, beachfront cafés, and easy access to Bali's offshore islands.

Many visitors choose Sanur because it provides:

Calm beaches suitable for families
Sunrise views over the ocean
Easy access to Nusa Penida
Easy access to Nusa Lembongan
Walking and cycling paths along the beachfront
Less traffic than Canggu and Ubud

Sanur is also home to the increasingly important Sanur Harbour, which serves thousands of daily passengers travelling to Bali's neighbouring islands.

2. Taxi from Bali Airport

Official airport taxis are available immediately outside arrivals.

Typical taxi costs:

Destination	Estimated Fare
Central Sanur	IDR 170,000–250,000
Sanur Harbour	IDR 180,000–260,000
Mertasari Area	IDR 200,000–280,000

Airport taxi counters generally provide fixed pricing before departure.

3. Grab and Gojek

Both Grab and Gojek operate from Bali Airport.

Pros:

Competitive pricing
App-based booking
Cashless payment options

Cons:

Possible waiting times
Surge pricing during busy periods
Pickup zones can change

For solo travellers and couples, ride-hailing apps can be a cost-effective solution.

4. Bluebird Taxi

Many travellers trust Bluebird Group because of its metered pricing and reputation.

Bluebird taxis are commonly used throughout Bali and can be an alternative to airport taxis when available.

Frequently Asked Questions
How long does it take to get from Ngurah Rai Airport to Sanur?

Most journeys take between 20 and 45 minutes depending on traffic conditions.

How much is a taxi from Bali Airport to Sanur?

Most airport taxis cost between IDR 170,000 and IDR 250,000. Prices vary based on destination and traffic conditions.

Is Sanur closer to Bali Airport than Ubud?

Yes. Sanur is significantly closer to Bali Airport than Ubud and generally requires less travel time.

Can I use Grab at Bali Airport?

Yes. Grab operates from designated pickup areas at Bali Airport.

Is Sanur a good place to stay in Bali?

Sanur is popular among families, couples, digital nomads and travellers heading to Nusa Penida due to its relaxed atmosphere and convenient location.

What is the best transfer option from Bali Airport to Sanur?

A private airport transfer is typically the most reliable and convenient option because it provides fixed pricing, luggage assistance and direct hotel drop-off.
