## How Far is Canggu from Bali Airport?

The distance between Ngurah Rai Airport and Canggu is approximately 16–20 kilometers depending on your destination area within Canggu.

Average travel times:

* Early morning: 30–50 minutes
* Normal traffic: 45–70 minutes
* Peak traffic: 90–120 minutes
* Busy holiday periods: up to 2+ hours possible

Traffic congestion around Canggu is one of the most common concerns mentioned by travelers visiting Bali.
